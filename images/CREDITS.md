## Formats

Each photo ships twice. The page loads the `.webp`; the `.jpg` is the fallback
an `onerror` handler swaps in for browsers that cannot decode WebP.

The `.webp` files were made from these originals: resampled to 1100px wide
(they display in roughly a 550px slot) and encoded with `cwebp -q 80`. That
took the six photos from 918 KB to 236 KB, 75% smaller. The `.jpg` files are
the untouched originals at their original size and are still the licensed
source above.
