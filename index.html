What it does

Client Powered sits next to the accounting software you already use and chases clients for the documents you're waiting on. Instead of a document request buried three replies deep in an email thread, each client gets one running list to complete, with reminders that go out on a schedule your firm sets and stop the moment the document arrives.

Who it's for

We built this for the firms that run tax season on email and good intentions.

Solo practitioners

One person doing it all, from the engagement letter to the extension.

Firms of 2 to 10

Small teams where document chasing competes with the billable work.

Tax, payroll and bookkeeping practices

Any mix of recurring client work that runs on paperwork you don't have yet.

How it works

  1. You set what each client owes you

    Tell Client Powered what you're waiting on for each client: a document, a signature, a form.

  2. Client Powered sends the reminders

    Reminders go out on the schedule you choose, to the client, not to you. They stop the moment the document arrives.

  3. The client uploads with no account needed

    The client opens a simple, secure link and uploads. No account, no password.

  4. The document lands on the client's record

    What comes back files itself against the right client record automatically.

Why I built this

I run a small accounting firm, and chasing down documents is the part of the job I dislike most: the third email about one bank statement, payroll hours that show up the morning payroll is due, a return that stalls for weeks on a single missing form. I built the tool I wanted for my own firm, and now a small group of other accounting firms is using it too. Frequently asked questions

When can I use it?

Client Powered is in private beta. Access is by request, and we bring in firms from the request list as beta spots open. We don't have a general release date to promise.

What does it cost?

It's free for firms in the beta. We'll set pricing later, and no firm will be charged without notice first.

Where does my client data live?

The beta runs on hosted infrastructure. Before your firm uploads any client documents, you'll get a written answer on exactly how data is stored and retained.

Does it replace my practice management or accounting software?

No. It sits alongside the software you already use and handles the document chase, not a replacement for it.

Who is building this?

A small team led by an accounting firm owner who is building the tool his own firm needed, listed in the Team section below.
