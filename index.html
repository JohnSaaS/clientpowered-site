What it does

Client Powered is meant to sit next to the accounting software you already use and answer one question: what does this client still owe me. Instead of a document request buried three replies deep in an email thread, each client will get one running list to complete, and your firm will get one running list to check.

Who it's for

We are building this for the firms that run tax season on email and good intentions.

Solo practitioners

One person doing it all, from the engagement letter to the extension.

Firms of 2 to 10

Small teams where document chasing competes with the billable work.

Tax, payroll and bookkeeping practices

Any mix of recurring client work that runs on paperwork you don't have yet.

What we are building

None of this exists today. This is what we are building toward, and the waitlist will help set the order we build it in.

Document requests that chase themselves

  • Reminders will go out on a schedule you set, not one you have to remember.
  • Clients will see exactly what is still open without emailing to ask.

One record for every client

  • Every request, note and deadline for a client will live in a single place.
  • No more piecing the story together from three email threads.

Deadline and job tracking across the firm

  • Every job and its deadline will be visible in one view across the firm.
  • Nothing will slip because no one happened to check.

A client portal without a password to lose

  • Clients will get in with a simple, secure link, not a password to reset.
  • Uploads will land directly on the right client record.

Reminders that stop on their own

  • The moment a document is uploaded, its reminder will stop automatically.
  • No more chasing something a client already sent.

One view of what every client still owes you

  • A single screen will show what is outstanding, across every client.
  • You will know where things stand without opening a single file.

How it works

  1. Set what each client owes you

    Tell Client Powered what you're waiting on for each client: a document, a signature, a form.

  2. Client Powered will send the reminders

    Reminders will go out on the schedule you choose, to the client, not to you.

  3. Documents will land in one place, tied to the client

    Whatever comes back will file itself against the right client record.

Why I'm building this

I run a small accounting firm, and chasing down documents is the part of the job I dislike most: the third email about one bank statement, payroll hours that show up the morning payroll is due, a return that stalls for weeks on a single missing form. I am building the tool I wish I already had. It doesn't exist yet, but I'm building it in the open, starting with the people who feel this the same way I do.

John Bowman, Founder
